1200x1000 preview images by script: upscale factor detected (ogre ~5.34x, goblin ~6.70x),
nearest-neighbour downsampled to native art pixels, the grey gradient background and the
"Pixel Based / Animated PNGs" badges removed, poses packed into uniform cells.

             row 0 run (2), row 1 attack with sword smear (2), row 2 idle (2)
