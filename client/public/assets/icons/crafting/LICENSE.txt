Sheet: 128 x 128 px, 4 x 4 grid of 32 x 32 px icons, RGBA.
  row 0: nails, wood plank, wooden rod, cloth
  row 1: copper ore, iron ore, silver nugget, gold nugget
  row 2: copper bar, iron bar, silver bar, gold bar
  row 3: feather, dragon scale, gem, leather

The file is used unmodified. RTS Agents maps its 28 crafting items onto these
16 cells (some cells are reused with a CSS hue/brightness filter) — see
client/src/utils/itemIcons.ts.
